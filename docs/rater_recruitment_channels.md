**Research and reporting only.** This finds *where* the 3-5 practitioner raters
described in `docs/rater_recruitment_outreach.md` might realistically be reached,
so recruitment is a targeted send, not a guess. It does **not** contact anyone,
sign up for any platform, or draft new outreach copy (the outreach message and
qualifying criteria already live in `rater_recruitment_outreach.md`). All
web-sourced facts are dated **2026-07-20** and cited at the end.

if started late. Practical read: start 1a and 1d **today**, not after a
week of waiting to see if they work - there is no fast paid fallback left
if they stall.

## The number that changes the strategy: n = 3-5, not 300

PROJECT_SPEC.md needs only **3-5 raters**. That is a *small-n, high-match* recruit, not
a survey. The implication is important and often missed: **broadcasting to a big
association or a subreddit is a worse fit than a handful of warm, targeted
asks.** You are not trying to fill a panel; you are trying to find 3-5 people who
(a) fit the profile from the outreach doc — real PM experience on complex /
infrastructure / public-sector / international-development programmes, comfortable
with risk registers, *not* an ML background — and (b) can give 4-6 hours inside
the Aug-2026 window. That profile is specific enough that *who you already have
one degree of separation from* beats *which channel is biggest*.

This memo is ordered accordingly: warm/targeted first, broadcast second, paid as
the reliable deadline fallback.

## 1. Free / organic channels

### 1a. Personal + faculty/alumni network (highest yield, fastest, free)
Not a "channel" with a URL, but realistically the **best first move** for n=3-5.
The profile wanted (infrastructure / public-sector / development PMs who work
with risk registers) is exactly the kind of person Madhu/Kruthik are one or two
introductions away from through:
- **University project-/programme-management programmes** — faculty and alumni
  are on-profile and reachable through a named contact, not a cold form.
  On-point programmes to consider approaching a contact at: Oxford Saïd **Major
  Programme Management** (MMPM), UCL Bartlett (infrastructure/project mgmt),
  Cranfield, and in the US Penn State and George Washington University PM
  programmes. Alumni of these skew senior and public-sector/infrastructure.
- **Ex-colleagues / PMO contacts** with delivery experience on
  multi-year programmes.
- **World Bank / MDB-adjacent development consultancies** — a deliberate echo of
  the corpus's own sources (the outreach doc already flags this).

Why first: best profile match, no platform fee, and the warm ask is what
actually converts at n=3-5. The limiting factor is simply whether such contacts
exist in reach — which is a Madhu/Kruthik question, not something research can
resolve.

### 1b. APM — Association for Project Management (UK) — strong fit for this corpus
The UK's chartered PM body, **40,000+ individual members and 480+ corporate
partnerships** (APM's own current "About us" page - corrected after
independent re-check; an earlier draft cited slightly stale 42,000/470
figures from a secondary source, understating the corporate count) —
and directly apt because a third of the corpus is UK government business cases
(HyNet, Padeswood, Free Breakfast Clubs). Real mechanisms, not just a homepage:
- **APM Community / regional networks** (e.g., the London Network) run events and
  have volunteer organisers who are reachable — a regional network is a concrete
  place to make a scoped ask rather than shouting into a general inbox.
- **Research Advisory Group (RAG)** — APM's research arm. Its remit is producing
  research *for* the profession more than *supplying participants*, so treat it as
  a credibility/contact route (an academic study on risk registers is squarely in
  its interest), not a ready participant pool.
- **Volunteering programme** — open to members *and non-members*; oriented to
  outreach/thought-leadership, so a rater ask is adjacent to, not exactly, its
  purpose. Best used to reach a network organiser who can forward the ask.
- Reality check: APM has no obvious "post your study, recruit participants"
  board. The value is warm reach into a UK PM population via a network contact,
  with a 2-4 week lead time to events/newsletters.

### 1c. PMI — Project Management Institute (global)
The largest global body. Mechanisms:
- **Local chapters** (many, worldwide) — chapter newsletters, LinkedIn pages, and
  meetups are the realistic route to members; a chapter officer can circulate a
  scoped ask.
- **PMI online community / discussion forums** — member Q&A/discussion space.
- Caveat: PMI's *official* research apparatus (Academic Sponsored Research, the
  College for Early Career Researchers, Thesis/Doctoral grants) is about
  **funding academic research and running PMI's own member surveys through a
  third party for anonymity** — it is *not* a "recruit raters for your study"
  service. So PMI = reach members via a chapter, not via an official participant
  pool. Expect 2-4+ weeks and uncertain yield.

### 1c-2. IPMA — International Project Management Association (added 2026-07-22)
Found while helping identify additional candidate sources after the
volunteer-only decision removed the paid fallback (see update note above) -
worth adding because it's more internationally-distributed than either APM
(UK-only) or PMI (US-centric-but-global), which fits a corpus spanning World
Bank projects across many countries. IPMA is a federation of **~70 national
Member Associations** across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas ([IPMA -
About Us](https://www.ipma.world/about-us), retrieved 2026-07-22) - each MA
develops PM competence and relationships with practitioners, corporations,
and government agencies in its own country. Same realistic mechanism as
APM/PMI: reach a national MA's own contact/volunteer route (most have
regional or national events and newsletters), not IPMA's central
secretariat. Worth checking whether any MA sits in a country this corpus's
projects are drawn from (e.g. Serbia, Peru, Mexico, Albania) for an
especially on-profile warm ask - same 2-4+ week chapter-cadence caveat as
APM/PMI applies.

### 1d. LinkedIn — targeted direct outreach (controllable, labour-intensive)
The most *controllable* organic channel for hitting the exact profile: search
"Project Manager" / "Programme Manager" / "PMO Lead" titles at World-Bank-adjacent
development consultancies and UK infrastructure delivery bodies (a deliberate echo
of the corpus). Send the outreach-doc message directly.
- Reality check: cold-outreach reply rates are low (~5-15%), so landing 3-5
  confirmed raters realistically means messaging **20-50+** well-targeted people.
  Doable in 1-3 weeks but it is real manual effort. Warm 2nd-degree intros (via
  1a) convert far better than cold 1st-contact.
- PM-focused LinkedIn groups exist but are noisy and low-conversion for a
  time-intensive ask; direct targeted messaging beats group posting here.

### 1e. Reddit r/projectmanagement and similar — low priority
Large, on-topic community, but **survey/research-recruitment posts are typically
rule-restricted** (mod approval and/or a designated weekly thread), and yield for
a 4-6 hour commitment from a genuinely senior, on-profile PM is unreliable. Fast
to post, but treat as a long shot, not a plan.

## 2. Paid channels (relevant only if the outreach doc's "paid?" question → yes)

`rater_recruitment_outreach.md` §"Two decisions" leaves *volunteer vs paid* open
and notes 4-6 hours is a substantial ask. If there is any budget, paid platforms
are the **most reliable way to hit the deadline**, because they exist specifically
to source hard-to-reach professionals fast. Per-participant cost (sourced
2026-07-20):

| Platform | Platform fee | Typical professional incentive | ~Total per completed rater | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Respondent.io** | $40/session pay-as-you-go (or $34 in a 63-session bundle) - confirmed exact | $100-$500 typical; **but Respondent's own incentive guide runs senior/executive remote up to ~$400-$800 and in-person up to ~$800 (Director) - $1,500 (C-level)** - corrected after independent re-check, an earlier draft capped this at $500 | **~$200-$700 typical, up to ~$1,500-2,000 if raters are senior/in-person** | Built for B2B professional recruiting; best profile-match odds for senior PMs, but a genuinely senior in-person rater can cost well above the typical range - budget the higher end if targeting infrastructure-programme-director-level profiles |
| **User Interviews** | $49-$98/session | $100-$500 | **~$200-$500** | Comparable to Respondent; large B2B pool |
| **Prolific** | 33.3% academic / 42.8% corporate platform fee | min £6/$8 per hr, recommend £9/$12+; premium/expert screeners cost extra | **~$80-$200** (4-6 hrs at a skilled rate + fee) | Academic-friendly pricing, BUT its pool skews general-population; genuine complex-infrastructure PMs are low-incidence, so **profile match is the risk**, not price |
| CleverX (alt) | Quote-based B2B panel | professional incentives | varies | Another B2B expert-panel option if the two above stall |

**Budget for the whole study (3-5 raters):**
- Respondent / User Interviews: ~**$800-$2,800** total typical (4 raters ×
  ~$200-$700), but could run to **~$4,000-$6,000** if raters skew senior/
  in-person on Respondent specifically (4 × ~$1,000-$1,500) - corrected after
  independent re-check of Respondent's incentive guide; ask which tier a
  screener targets before assuming the lower figure. Fast and reliable, but
  real money either way.
- Prolific: ~**$320-$800** total — cheapest, but you may burn screening cost and
  still not find on-profile senior PMs; better as a supplement than the sole
  source.
- **Cost-guard note:** PROJECT_SPEC.md's $30 threshold is specifically about **API
  spend**, so it does not literally bind here — but the project's cost-awareness
  posture does. This is a *separate* budget line the project owners must approve,
  and it is the concrete lever behind the outreach doc's volunteer-vs-paid
  decision: even a **small honorarium/gift card** (well under the paid-platform
  totals) attached to an *organic* ask (1a-1d) materially widens who says yes and
  speeds conversion, without paying full marketplace rates.

## 3. Realistic timelines to 3-5 *confirmed* raters

This is the thing that actually matters against the end-of-Aug-2026 deadline
(~5-6 weeks out from 2026-07-20). "Confirmed and available for the window," not
"posted."

| Channel | Time to 3-5 confirmed | Effort | Profile match | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1a. Personal / faculty-alumni network** | **days-2 weeks** (if contacts exist) | Low-med | **Best** | Free (or small honorarium) |
| **2. Paid platform** (Respondent / User Interviews) | **~3-10 days** (small B2B study fills fast unless the screener is very narrow) | Low (they do the sourcing) | Good (Respondent/UI); risky (Prolific) | $800-$2,800 typical, up to ~$6,000 if senior/in-person |
| **1d. LinkedIn targeted outreach** | 1-3 weeks (must message 20-50+) | **High** (manual) | Good if targeted | Free |
| **1b/1c. APM / PMI via a network or chapter** | 2-4+ weeks (event/newsletter cadence, moderation) | Med | Good | Free |
| **1e. Reddit** | days to post, yield unreliable | Low | Uncertain | Free |

**Read against the deadline:** only **1a (warm network)** and **2 (paid
platform)** reliably deliver confirmed, on-profile raters inside a ~5-6 week
window. Association and LinkedIn routes can work but carry real schedule risk
this close to the deadline.
