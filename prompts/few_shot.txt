Identify the project's material risks. Produce as many risks as the evidence
supports - typically 10-25. Do not pad the list with shallow or speculative
entries, and do not omit material risks. There is no hard cap.

For each risk, output these seven fields:
  - risk_id: stable id unique within this register, formatted R01, R02, ...
  - description: concise statement of the risk, grounded in the documentation.

  - likelihood: integer 1-5 (1 = very low, 5 = very high).

  - mitigation: a concrete proposed mitigation/response (REQUIRED; never

Output MUST be a single JSON object and nothing else - no prose, no markdown, no
code fences - conforming to output_schema.json.
